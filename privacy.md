# TravelDar Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 26 October 2026_

TravelDar ("the app") is a private travel journal. **We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data on our servers — the app has no servers, no accounts, no advertising and no analytics.** Everything you enter or the app detects stays on your own device.

## What the app stores on your device
- **Trips and countries**: the countries and dates of your trips (entered by you or detected), optional city and notes.
- **Settings**: your home country and display preferences.
- **Location readings** (only if you turn on automatic tracking): rounded to about 11 km and used only to work out which country you are in. They are deleted automatically after 90 days; the resulting trips remain until you delete them.

## Location
If you enable automatic tracking, TravelDar uses your location while the app is open to detect the country you are in, using map data bundled inside the app. No network request is made to look up a country. Optional background tracking uses Apple's low-power significant-location-change service and requires "Always" location access; you can turn it off at any time. Location is never sent anywhere.

## Calendar (optional)
If you turn on "Add trips to device calendar", TravelDar asks for calendar access and adds all-day events for your trips to a calendar named "TravelDar" on your device. The app does not read or change your other calendars' events. This data stays in your device's calendar (and in your own iCloud if you use iCloud calendars, under Apple's terms).

## Widget
The home-screen widget shows your next trip and travel statistics. The data it shows is shared with the widget only through an App Group on your device.

## Exporting and deleting your data
You can export your trips as JSON or CSV from the Profile tab, and delete all travel data at any time ("Delete all travel data"). Deleting the app removes its data from your device.

## Children
The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
