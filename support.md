# TravelDar Support

Need help or found a bug? Email **kaareyes.developer@gmail.com** with your iPhone model, iOS version and a short description.

## Common questions

**Why isn't a country detected automatically?**
Automatic tracking needs location permission (Profile → Automatic tracking). Fixes near borders or at sea are marked uncertain and need a second reading before they count. Background tracking is best-effort: iOS may delay it, and it does not run after you force-quit the app. You can always add or correct a trip by hand with the + button.

**Does TravelDar upload my location?**
No. Everything stays on your device. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).

**How do I get trips into my iPhone calendar?**
Profile → Calendar → "Add trips to device calendar", then allow calendar access. If it says access is off, open Settings → TravelDar → Calendars.

**How do I remove my data?**
Profile → Data & Privacy → Delete all travel data.
